# Messaging framework · Singha Tax Consultants · 2026-10-27

**Summary**
- **Positioning:** the fixed-price, Hindi-friendly, CA-checked compliance team for West Delhi's shops and small businesses.
- **Value prop (10 words):** *GST and ITR filed on time, at a fixed price.*
- **Lead segment:** shopkeepers and traders within ~5 km of Sagarpur (GST retainers = recurring revenue). Seasonal second: ITR filers (belated ITR now, the big season in June–July).
- Customer language here is still **hypothesis** [Estimate]. Replace it with real phrases after the 10 client interviews.

## Positioning (April Dunford method)
| Step | Answer |
|---|---|
| Competitive alternatives | Neighbourhood munim · local CA firms with "onwards" pricing · DIY on the GST portal · online platforms (ClearTax, Vakilsearch) |
| Unique attributes | Published fixed price (₹1,499/qtr GST) · every return CA-reviewed (CA Prateek Arora) · Hindi/Hinglish explanations · WhatsApp-first with filing proof · local office in Sagarpur · free tools (compliance calendar, calculators) |
| Value they enable | No missed due dates or late fees · no price surprises · understand what was filed · a real person nearby when a notice comes |
| Who cares most | Small shopkeepers, traders, boutiques, clinics in Sagarpur / Dwarka Mor / Uttam Nagar / Tilak Nagar / Palam who have been burned by an unreliable munim or a surprise notice |
| Market category | "Local GST & tax filing service for small businesses" (not "CA firm"; that invites comparison on pedigree, where older firms win) |

**Positioning statement:**
> For shopkeepers and small businesses in West Delhi who are tired of chasing their munim and worrying about GST notices, **Singha Tax Consultants** is the local filing team in Sagarpur that files every return on time at a fixed, published price, explains it in Hindi, and sends proof on WhatsApp, with every return checked by CA Prateek Arora. Unlike local CA firms that quote "₹1,5xx onwards" or online platforms with no one nearby, we're upfront and around the corner.

## Value proposition
- **10 words:** GST and ITR filed on time, at a fixed price.
- **Hinglish:** Time pe filing, fixed price, har return CA-checked.

We remind you before the due date, file, and send you the proof on WhatsApp. CA Prateek Arora checks every return. And if a notice comes, we explain it in Hindi and reply."
