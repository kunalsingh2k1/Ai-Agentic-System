# Offers · Singha Tax Consultants · 2026-10-27

**Summary**
- 1,000 new clients in 22 weeks needs **low-friction entry offers**. Most will start with one small, specific job, then convert to GST retainers.
- Diagnosis: the core offer is good value (₹1,499/qtr), but its **weakest factor is perceived likelihood**: zero reviews, zero proof. The second weakest is effort: only 2 prices are published, everything else is "quote after a call".
- Recommended: 4 entry offers + 1 service guarantee. **Prices marked ⚠ are proposals for the owner to set**, not existing prices.

## Value-equation diagnosis
| Factor | Today | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome | "Never miss a due date": strong | Keep; make it "Due date humari zimmedari" |
| Perceived likelihood | **Weak**: no reviews, empty testimonials | Reviews drive, filing-proof screenshots, named CA, service guarantee |
| Time delay | OK | "Filed within 48 hours of documents" (only if deliverable) |
| Effort / friction | **Weak-medium**: most services need a call for a quote | Publish "from" prices for ITR and bundles; WhatsApp document checklist |

## Entry offers
### 1. Free GST Health Check (all year, lead magnet)
- **What:** a 15-minute review of the last 2 quarters' GSTR-1/3B, ITC mismatch check vs GSTR-2B, late-fee check. On WhatsApp or at the shop.
- **Output:** a 1-page "health card" (green/amber/red) with what to fix.
- **Why:** gives real value first; perfect for door-to-door and market visits; natural path to the ₹1,499/qtr retainer.
- **Capacity guard:** cap at 15 checks/week until help is hired.

### 2. Belated ITR Rescue (now → 31 Dec 2026 [verify date])
- **What:** belated or revised ITR for AY 2026-27 with AIS/26AS check, CA review, filing proof on WhatsApp.
- **Price:** ⚠ publish a fixed "from" price. Proposal: ITR-1/2 **₹999**, ITR-3/4 **₹1,999** (BMC shows ITR "from ₹1,000" [Research]).
- **Urgency:** real deadline, so no fake scarcity needed.
- **Follow-on:** advance tax reminder (15 Mar) → next year's ITR (June–July).

### 3. Dukaan Starter Pack (new shops, all year)
- **What:** GST registration + Udyam + first quarter's GST filing + compliance calendar set up on WhatsApp.
- **Price:** ⚠ proposal ₹2,999 (vs ₹1,499 + ₹1,499 + Udyam separately). Government fees extra.
- **Why:** new registrants are a growing pool (Delhi GST base +13% YoY [Research]).

### 4. Switch-to-Singha first quarter (shops leaving another filer)
- **What:** we take over from your current munim/CA, check past filings, and file your next quarter.
- **Price:** ⚠ proposal: first quarter **₹999** instead of ₹1,499, then the regular price. Margin impact: −₹500 once per client.
- **Condition:** 2-quarter minimum.

### Service guarantee (applies to GST retainers)
> "Filing proof on WhatsApp before the due date, or that quarter's fee is waived. (Documents must reach us 3 days before the due date.)"
- It's a promise about our own work, not about tax outcomes. ⚠ CA Prateek to confirm it's OK under ICAI norms and that delivery capacity supports it.

## Referral offer
- **Client referral:** "Dost ko laiye: unko pehla quarter ₹999, aapko next quarter ₹500 off." Both sides get a credit.
- ⚠ **Compliance check:** ICAI members may not pay or share fees for client referrals. The offer is a discount/credit from the Pvt Ltd company, not a commission paid by a CA. **CA Prateek to confirm** before launch, and before any paid partner (munim, agent) scheme.

## Pricing recommendations (light; full `/pricing` later)
1. Publish "from" prices for ITR and the bundles. "Quote after a call" is friction, and competitors show "from ₹1,000" [Research].
2. Show yearly cost next to quarterly: "₹1,499/quarter = ₹5,996/year, all GSTR-1 & 3B included."
3. Keep government fees and GST on fees clearly "extra", as today.
