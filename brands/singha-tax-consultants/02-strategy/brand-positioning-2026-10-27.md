# Brand voice guide · Singha Tax Consultants · 2026-10-27

**Summary**
- **What we stand for:** *"Due date humari zimmedari."* The firm owns the deadline so the shopkeeper doesn't have to.
- **The one thing we own:** a fixed, published price + Hindi/Hinglish explanations + filing proof on WhatsApp, reviewed by a CA. No local competitor offers all of these [Research: competitor-profiling-2026-10-26].
- **Voice:** clear, calm, local, upfront. We sound like a trusted neighbour who knows tax, not like a corporate CA firm.
- **The enemy:** the old way. Chasing your munim on the 19th, surprise late fees, "price onwards", notices nobody explains.

## Brand core
| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Make tax and compliance stress-free for small businesses in West Delhi |
| **Promise** | Your filing is done on time, checked by a CA, at the price we told you, with proof on WhatsApp |
| **Values** | **Careful** (CA review on every return, we double-check) · **Clear** (we explain in your language; no jargon without a translation) · **Reliable** (we remind you before you remember) |
| **Personality** | Archetype: *the Caregiver + Sage*. Traits: dependable, plain-spoken, warm |

## Differentiation
- **Own:** "On time, fixed price, explained in Hindi, CA-checked."
- **Proof we own it:**
  - ₹1,499/quarter GST price published on the website
  - CA Prateek Arora reviews returns (owner-approved to name)
  - Office in Sagarpur
  - WhatsApp-first contact
- **Proof still missing (build it):**
  - Google reviews
  - Client testimonials
  - A "filed on time" record, e.g. "312 returns filed before due date this quarter". Only use real counts.

## Voice traits
| We are | Not | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clear** | Jargon-heavy | "GSTR-3B matlab har mahine ka tax summary, jo 20 tarikh tak file hota hai." | "Ensure timely compliance with Section 39 return obligations." |
| **Calm** | Fear-selling | "Notice aaya? Pehle padhte hain, phir sahi jawab dete hain." | "NOTICE = JAIL?! Act now!!" |
| **Local** | Corporate | "Sagarpur office mein aaiye, chai pe baat karte hain." | "Our pan-India platform leverages synergies…" |
| **Upfront** | Salesy | "GST return: ₹1,499 per quarter. Bas." | "Best rates! Call for amazing offer!" |

## Vocabulary
- **Use:**
  - due date
  - time pe
  - fixed price
  - CA-checked
  - WhatsApp pe proof
  - dukaan
  - tension-free
  - hum yaad dilayenge
  - samjhayenge
  - notice ka jawab
- **Avoid:**
  - "best CA" / "No. 1" (ICAI norms + unprovable)
  - "guaranteed refund"
  - "zero tax"
  - "hassle-free" (everyone says it)
  - "solutions"
  - "synergy"
  - fear words like "jail" or "raid"
