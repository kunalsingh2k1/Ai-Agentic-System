# Marketing plan · Singha Tax Consultants · 27 Oct 2026 → 31 Mar 2027

**Summary**
- **Goal:** 1,000 new clients by 31 Mar 2027 (owner decision). That's 22 weeks, averaging 45 clients/week and rising from ~15/week now to ~75/week in March.
- **Engine:** free and near-free local channels. **Offline market clusters + Google Maps/reviews + referrals**, with Instagram for trust and ₹2,000/month as a paid experiment.
- **Honest math [Estimate]:**
  - With the current team (owner 30 hrs/week, no extra help), the realistic base case is **300–450 clients** by 31 Mar.
  - Reaching 1,000 needs three **unlocks**: (1) a commission-based field helper by mid-December, (2) at least 2 partner channels live by January, (3) budget stepped up to ₹5,000 from December, paid for by new revenue.
- **Checkpoint gates** on 30 Nov, 31 Dec and 31 Jan decide whether we scale, fix, or re-set the date. They protect the business from chasing a number that the delivery team can't serve.

## 3. Phases
### Phase 1: Foundation (27 Oct – 23 Nov, weeks 1–4)
Build trust assets and the offline motion.
- **Trust assets:**
  - GBP live
  - 25 reviews
  - Testimonials section fixed
  - Justdial, Sulekha, Bing, Apple, Facebook listings
  - Search Console + GA4
- **WhatsApp Business:** catalogue (4 offers), quick replies, labels (New lead / Health check / Client / Do not contact), review-link template, 60-minute reply promise.
- **Offers:** CA Prateek approves offers and guarantee wording; ITR "from" prices published.
- **Offline:** 2 market walks/week (Sagarpur Sangam Market first, then Dwarka Mor), 40 shops/week, Free GST Health Check.
- **Content:** 4 carousels + 2 Reels/week. Belated ITR series starts.
- **Measure:** "How did you hear about us?" on every lead, logged in the office Leads/Tasks.

### Phase 2: Traction (24 Nov – 4 Jan, weeks 5–10)
Win the 31 December window and start referrals.
- **Belated ITR Rescue campaign** (carousels, WhatsApp status, flyers, Meta ad 3 km) until 31 Dec. Then GSTR-9 annual return for existing GST clients (upsell).
- **Referral offer** live (after CA approval); asked in every filing-proof message.
- **Locality pages** live: Sagarpur, Dwarka Mor, Uttam Nagar (week 6), Tilak Nagar, Palam (week 8).
- **Field helper** (commission per client won) from mid-December, if Gate 1 passes.
- **Partner outreach:** market associations (vyapar mandal), local insurance/real-estate agents, banks' small-business desks. 10 conversations, 2 live by early January. ⚠ Any paid partner scheme needs CA Prateek's ICAI check.

### Phase 3: Scale (5 Jan – 31 Mar, weeks 11–22)
Repeat what works, add capacity.
- Scale market clusters to Uttam Nagar and Tilak Nagar markets (4 walks/week with helper).
- **Dukaan Starter Pack** push (new shops), **Switch-to-Singha** offer for health-check follow-ups.
- March: advance tax (15 Mar) and year-end tax planning for clinics/professionals; book ITR clients for June–July.
- Budget at ₹5,000: Google local search ads ("GST return filing near me", "CA near me" in 5 km).
- **Delivery capacity:** 1 part-time filer when GST clients reach ~150 (likely Dec), a 2nd at ~400 (likely Feb). Not marketing spend, but required.

## 9. Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Delivery capacity breaks: late filings ruin the "on time" promise | Filer hiring triggers; cap health checks per week; AccountX automation |
| ICAI issues (advertising a CA, referral fees, solicitation) | CA Prateek signs off on offers, guarantee and referral wording; no "best CA" claims; field outreach done by the company/owner |
| Low conversion from offline | Health card with clear fixes; Switch offer; follow up within 24 hours |
| Wrong due dates in content (new Income-tax Act) | Every tax post fact-checked by CA Prateek (office Quality Checker flags dates) |
| Owner burnout at 5 hrs/day | Batch content weekly in the office; helper from December |
| Spam complaints on WhatsApp | Opt-in broadcasts only; "Do not contact" respected (enforced in the office) |
