# SEO audit · singhatax.com · 2026-10-26

**Summary**
- **The technical foundation is good** [Research: crawled 9 pages, robots.txt, sitemap.xml]. Pages are pre-rendered HTML, titles and descriptions are well written, canonicals are set, Open Graph is present, `AccountingService` schema is in place and HTTPS → www redirects work.
- **Local SEO is close to zero.**
  - No Google Business Profile.
  - Not found on Justdial or Sulekha.
  - Uttam Nagar, Dwarka (Mor), Tilak Nagar and Palam are mentioned **0 times** on the 9 pages checked; only Sagarpur appears (in the address).
- **Content is thin.** There are 3 blog posts, all in English, and the "What our clients say" section on the homepage is **empty**.
- **The brand doesn't surface in web search**, even for "Singha Tax" [Research, low confidence: the search tool is US-based]. Confirm with Google Search Console.
