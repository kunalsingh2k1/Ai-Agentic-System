# Brand report · Singha Tax Consultants · 26 Oct 2026

## 1. Executive summary
- **Strong product, invisible brand.**
  - What works: CA-reviewed service, a published low GST price (₹1,499/quarter) and a well-built website with free tools.
  - What doesn't: no Google Business Profile, no reviews, no local pages, no directory listings. Locals searching "CA near me" can't find Singha.
- **Biggest problem: trust signals are zero.** The homepage has an empty "What our clients say" section, and every competitor shows testimonials or "1000+ clients".
- **Biggest opportunity: the open space in local search plus Hindi/Hinglish.** All 7+ local competitors are English-only and hide their prices. Singha can be *the fixed-price, Hindi-friendly CA team for West Delhi's shops*.
- **Goal reality:** 1,000 **new** clients by 31 Mar 2027 means ~45/week from today, on ₹2,000/month and a 2-person team [Estimate]. Reachable only with a free-channel engine and a hiring or automation step for delivery. A **31 Jul 2027 (ITR deadline)** finish line is far more realistic. Decision pending.
- **Do first (this week):** create the Google Business Profile → ask every existing client for a Google review → fix the empty testimonials section → verify Google Search Console.

## 3. Market & audience
- Delhi: **9.22 lakh active GST taxpayers** (Jun 2026), +13% in a year [Research: GSTN]. West + SW Delhi ≈ **2 lakh** [Estimate]. 1,000 clients is ~0.5% of that.
- Priority segments (hypotheses, to validate with 10 client interviews):
  1. **Shopkeepers/traders**: fear of notices and late fees; objection "munim is cheaper"
  2. **Boutiques/retail**: registrations + GST + ITR-4
  3. **Clinics/professionals**: 44ADA, TDS, advance tax
  4. **SMEs/startups/agencies**: ROC, TDS, bookkeeping
  5. **Salaried** (seasonal, Jun–Jul)
- **Demand calendar:**
  - **31 Dec**: belated/revised ITR + GSTR-9 (the window is open now)
  - Monthly: GST due dates on the 11th and 20th

  - Verify all dates before posting
