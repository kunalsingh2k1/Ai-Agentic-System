# Competitor profiling · Singha Tax Consultants · 2026-10-26

**Summary**
- **CA Alok Kumar (Dwarka Sec 4) is the benchmark** [Research]. It has 25+ service pages, locality sections for every target area (Uttam Nagar, Palam, Sagarpur, Tilak Nagar, Dwarka Mor), named testimonials by locality, free tools, a "1000+ happy clients" claim and a 60-minute response promise.
- **Every competitor is English-only.** None publishes complete, firm prices (CA Alok's prices show "₹1,5xx onwards" placeholders; BMC shows "from").
- **Singha's openings:** Hindi/Hinglish, fixed published prices, a shopkeeper-first offer, and fast WhatsApp response.

All facts are from each firm's own site, fetched or searched on 2026-10-26 [Research]. Claims like "4.9★" or "1000+ clients" are unverified.

## Profile cards

### 1. CA Alok Kumar: Dwarka Sector 4 (also Rajendra Place)
- **Promise:** "Dwarka's Trusted Chartered Accountant. 20+ Years of Tax, Audit & Advisory Excellence."
- **Segments:** salaried, MSMEs, startups, NRIs, HNIs, litigation clients
- **Differentiators:** FCA, LLM, litigation depth, "110+ professional team", "100% digital delivery", says it advises on the Income Tax Act 2025
- **Prices:** "ITR ₹1,xxx onwards", "GST ₹1,5xx onwards", notice reply ₹7,5xx+ (placeholders; the FAQ contradicts the cards)
- **Proof:** "1000+ happy clients", "4.9★" (no source), 6 testimonials tagged by locality ("Trader, Dwarka Sector 12")
- **CTAs:** call, WhatsApp, free 15-min consultation, "response within 60 minutes"
- **Lead tools:** ITR form finder, GST late-fee calculator, CA fee estimator, deadline checker
- **Local SEO:** names every Dwarka sector plus Uttam Nagar, Palam, Sagarpur, Najafgarh, Janakpuri, Tilak Nagar, Rajouri Garden (sections, not separate URLs)
- **Weak spots:** English only; placeholder prices look unfinished; positioned premium/litigation, so it's less attractive to a small shopkeeper
- **How to beat:** be the friendly, fixed-price, Hindi-speaking firm for small shops; build real locality pages; match the 60-minute WhatsApp response
