# Social media competitor & viral-content analysis · Singha Tax Consultants · 2026-10-26

**Summary**
- **Local competitors are nearly absent on social media.**
  - 7 of 16 West Delhi CA/tax firms link any social account on their website.
  - Only **one (PK Vats & Co., Dwarka) posts Shorts regularly**: median **~1,000 views** per Short, best 23,000 [Data: 48 recent Shorts].
  - The strongest web competitor, CA Alok Kumar, links no social accounts at all.
  - **The local social space is open.**
- **The real benchmark is Indian tax creators.** Across **2,157 YouTube items (1,622 Shorts)** from 30 Hindi/English searches and 17 creator channels, tax Shorts reach **0.5–19 million views**. The median tax Short gets ~6,000 [Data].
- **What travels:**
  1. **Hindi (Devanagari) titles:** median **15,500 vs 4,750** views for non-Devanagari titles (3.3×).
  2. **"Kitna tax?" calculations** (median 28,000) and **step-by-step how-tos** (median 19,000) are the most *reliable* topics.
  3. **Notice / UPI / cash "fear-check"** topics have lower medians but produce the **biggest hits** (2.4M–19M) and the **highest like rates (5–7%)**.
- **Biggest opening for Singha:** the shopkeeper's fear of **UPI-linked GST/income-tax notices**. Kirana shopkeepers tearing down UPI QR codes over notices got **19M views and 622K likes** [Data]. Yet no local firm makes calm, local, Hindi answers to it.
- **Instagram:** follower counts and Reel views sit behind a login, so they couldn't be measured. Most of these creators post the same video as a Reel and a Short, so the YouTube numbers are a reasonable stand-in [Estimate].

## 1. Method & limits
| Source | What was collected | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Competitor websites (16) | Social links in the page HTML | High for what's linked; some sites render links with JavaScript and may hide them |
| YouTube search (30 queries × all/short filter) | Title, views, length, channel (via public oEmbed) | High: public counts. Search ranking favours already-popular videos (survivorship bias) |
| YouTube creator Shorts tabs (17 channels × ~48 recent Shorts) | Views per Short → each creator's median → **outlier score** (views ÷ median) | High. Outliers show formats that beat the creator's normal reach |
| Watch pages of 16 viral Shorts | Exact views + likes | High |
| Instagram / Facebook / LinkedIn | Handles from websites + web search | **Low**: login walls; no follower or Reel metrics |

## 6. Patterns to copy (and what to avoid)
1. **Hook = one personal risk or one everyday question in the first line, in Hindi.** "…करने पर आ सकता है Notice?", "Kitna cash rakh sakte hain?", "…पर Tax का झटका!".
2. **Everyday money moments beat tax jargon.** UPI, cash at home, bank transfers, gold, salary. The tax angle comes second.
3. **Numbers make it concrete:** ₹40 lakh, ₹2 lakh cash, ₹12 lakh salary, but only alongside a clear question.
4. **Timeliness wins spikes:** "new rules from 1 November", deadline extended, GST council updates. CA Guru Ji does this every month.
5. **How-to = steady views and saves:** nil GST return, Udyam, GST registration, ITR online.
6. **Emotion drives engagement:** fear-check topics get 5–7% likes vs 1–3% for how-tos.
7. **Avoid:**
   - Fear without an answer. It conflicts with Singha's "calm, not scary" voice and with ICAI norms.
   - "Zero tax" clickbait promises.
   - English-only titles for the shopkeeper audience.
   - Copying anyone's script or footage. Use the format, write original content.

## 7. What it means for Singha
- **Position:** *"Notice aaya? Ghabraiye mat."* Take the highest-emotion topic (UPI/cash/notice) and answer it **calmly, locally, in Hindi**. That's Singha's voice (02-strategy/brand-positioning) plus the format that performs.
- **Format mix (6/week, cross-posted to Reels + Shorts + Facebook):**
  - 2 fear-check answers (notice / UPI / cash)
  - 2 how-tos (nil return, GST registration, Udyam)
  - 1 "kitna tax / kitna fee" calculation
  - 1 timely rules round-up or deadline post
- **Titles and on-screen text:** Devanagari first line + Hinglish caption.
- **Local edge:** end every video with "Sagarpur office · WhatsApp 93552 72079". No big creator can say "aapke paas".
- **Measure:** track views per post vs the account median (outlier score) in the office scorecard; double down on any format that beats 3×.
