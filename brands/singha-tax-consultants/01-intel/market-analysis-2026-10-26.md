# Market analysis · Singha Tax Consultants · 2026-10-26

**Summary**
- Delhi has **9.22 lakh active GST taxpayers** [Research: GSTN "9 Years of GST" report, base date 30 Jun 2026]. That's up from 8.13 lakh in May 2025 [Research: SBI Research, Jul 2025], about 13% growth in a year.
- West + South-West Delhi probably hold **about 2 lakh** of them [Estimate: the two districts have ~22% of Delhi's population, applied to the GST base]. 1,000 new clients is **about 0.5%** of that base.
- The market is **crowded and undifferentiated**: dozens of CA firms all claim "best CA in Dwarka / West Delhi" in English. Nobody owns **Hindi/Hinglish**, **transparent low prices**, or **shopkeeper-first** service.
- Demand is **highly seasonal**. GST is monthly or quarterly and steady; ITR peaks June–July. A 31 March deadline misses the ITR peak.

## Market definition
Tax and compliance services (GST, ITR, TDS, ROC, registrations, bookkeeping) for **small businesses and professionals** in **Sagarpur, Dwarka Mor, Uttam Nagar, Tilak Nagar, Palam**, with all of West + SW Delhi as the wider ring. Pan-India online is a secondary market.

## Trends that matter
1. **GST base keeps growing** (~9–13%/yr) [Research]. New registrants need a filer from day one, so a **GST registration + first-year filing bundle** is a natural entry offer.
2. **GST 2.0 rate changes (5% / 18% / 40%)** [Research: singhatax.com calculator page]. Confusion creates demand for awareness content and the calculator.
3. **New Income-tax Act 2025** [Research, verify]. Creates "what changed" questions for every ITR filer. It's a strong evergreen content pillar for 2026–27.
4. **Notices driven by UPI and bank data** [Research: Karnataka traders received ~18,000 GST notices tied to UPI receipts]. Fear of notices is a top trigger for shopkeepers.
5. **Local search has moved to Google Maps** ("CA near me") and WhatsApp-first contact. Competitors with Maps listings and reviews capture these leads.
