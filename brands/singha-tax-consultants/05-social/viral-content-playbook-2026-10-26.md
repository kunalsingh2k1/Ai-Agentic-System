# Viral content playbook · Singha Tax Consultants · 2026-10-26

**Summary**
- 15 Reel/Short concepts. Each one is modelled on a **proven viral format** from `01-intel/social-competitor-analysis-2026-10-26.md` (the reference views are real), and rewritten in Singha's voice: calm, local, Hindi-first, fixed price.
- **Do not copy** any creator's script, footage or audio. These are original scripts using the same *format*.
- **Fact-check before posting:** every limit, section, date or fee marked [verify] needs CA Prateek's check. The law changed with the Income-tax Act 2025.
- **Specs:**
  - 9:16, 25–45 seconds, face to camera (Meenakshi or CA Prateek) or a text-on-screen carousel.
  - Burned-in Hinglish captions; first line on screen in Devanagari.
  - Post as a Reel + Short + Facebook Reel. Build them in Carousel Studio or with the `personal/singha-reel-editor` skill.

## Posting rules
- **Cadence:** 6/week.
  - Mon: fear-check
  - Tue: how-to
  - Wed: fear-check
  - Thu: kitna tax/fee
  - Fri: how-to
  - Sat: rules round-up or local proof
- **Hook (0–2 s):** one personal risk or one everyday question. Text on screen = spoken line.
- **Answer (2–25 s):** 3 points max. Calm tone, no "jail/raid" words.
- **Local close (last 5 s):** "Sagarpur office · WhatsApp 93552 72079 · Free GST Health Check". Pin a comment with the WhatsApp link.
- **Title / first caption line:** Devanagari (3.3× median views in the data), then Hinglish.
- **Track:** views ÷ account median. Re-make any format that beats 3×.

## The 15 concepts
### A. Notice / UPI / cash fear-check (highest emotion; answer calmly)
**1. "UPI से पेमेंट लेते हो? GST notice आ सकता है?"**
- Format ref: "UPI Payments पर Tax का झटका!" 2.9M · "Tearing UPI QR" 19M
- Hook: "Dukaan pe UPI QR lagaya hai? Notice ka darr hai? Pehle yeh sun lo."
- Beats:
  1. UPI se paisa lena galat nahi hai.
  2. Notice tab aata hai jab UPI receipts aur GST/ITR mein bataya turnover match nahi karta.
  3. 3 cheezein check karo: turnover limit [verify], GST registration, return mein sahi turnover.
- CTA: "Free GST Health Check: WhatsApp 93552 72079."
- Why: the biggest shopkeeper fear in the data, and no local firm answers it.

**2. "एक account से दूसरे में पैसा डाला, notice आएगा?"**
- Format ref: CA Sumit Sharma 130K (25× his median)
- Beats: own-account transfers are not income → but large cash deposits and unexplained credits get flagged (AIS) [verify limits] → keep a record.
- CTA: "AIS mein kuch ajeeb dikha? Humein bhejiye."

**3. "घर में कितना cash रख सकते हो? (Shopkeeper edition)"**
- Format ref: warikoo 1.2M
- Beats: no fixed limit on keeping cash, but the source must be explained → limits on accepting cash (section 269ST, ₹2 lakh [verify]) → business cash book matters.

**4. "GST notice आया? पहले ये 3 काम करो"**
- Format ref: "Income Tax NOTICE se सावधान ⚠️" 2.4M
- Beats:
  1. Don't panic, read the type and due date.
  2. Don't ignore it: reply within time.
  3. Don't reply without matching GSTR-1/3B/2B.
- Close: "Notice ka jawab hum Hindi mein samjhakar dete hain."

**5. "ये 5 गलतियाँ = पक्का notice"**
- Format ref: "Income Tax और GST से Notice आयेगा, अगर ये किया तो" 3.8M (6% likes)
- Beats: late returns · turnover mismatch (UPI vs return) · fake/unfiled supplier ITC · cash sales not reported · wrong ITR form for business. On-screen counter 1→5.

### B. Step-by-step how-to (steady views, saves)
**6. "Nil GST return 2 मिनट में कैसे भरें"**
- Format ref: "शून्य जीएसटी रिटर्न कैसे दाखिल करें" 560K
- Screen recording of the portal (blur the GSTIN). Ends: "Har mahine ka jhanjhat nahi chahiye? ₹1,499/quarter mein hum karte hain."

**7. "नई दुकान? GST registration चाहिए या नहीं?"**
- Format ref: ISHAN LLB GST registration 1.8M (long-form)
- Beats: turnover thresholds [verify: goods vs services] → when it's mandatory anyway (e-commerce, interstate) → documents list.
- CTA: Dukaan Starter Pack (after the owner sets the price).

**8. "Udyam (MSME) certificate free में, ये फायदे"**
- Format ref: Sarkari DNA 1.6M
- Beats: free on the government portal → 3 benefits → "Hum bhi kar dete hain, Sagarpur office se."

**9. "Belated ITR अभी भी भर सकते हो: last date 31 Dec"** [verify]
- Format ref: MyOnlineCA "ITR Filing Online" 690K + deadline spikes
- Beats: who missed 31 July → what happens if you file now (late fee [verify]) → documents. Run weekly until 31 Dec with a countdown.

**10. "GSTR-1 vs GSTR-3B: फर्क 30 सेकंड में"**
- Format ref: PK Vats "GST vs Income Tax difference" 23K (23× median: the local proof)
- Split-screen comparison card; ends with the due dates (11th / 20th).

### C. "Kitna tax / kitna fee?" calculations (most reliable cluster: median 28K)
**11. "दुकान का turnover ₹40 लाख: कितना tax?"**
- Format ref: Sakchi Jain "zero taxes on 12 lakh salary" 9.6M · "5 crore income par tax kitna" 3.9M
- Presumptive taxation 44AD example [verify rates/limits] with a simple on-screen table. "Aapka number WhatsApp karo, hum calculate karke bhejenge."

**12. "Doctor/clinic: ₹30 लाख कमाई पर कितना tax? (44ADA)"** [verify]
- Targets the clinics segment; same table format.

**13. "CA की fees कितनी होती है? हमारी: ₹1,499/quarter"**
- Turns the published price into content. "Per year ₹5,996, sab GSTR-1 & 3B included." Upfront-price pillar.

### D. Timely and local
**14. "1 नवंबर से बदले ये नियम (GST/Tax/Bank)"**
- Format ref: CA Guru Ji "1 October 2026 New Rules" 110K · "Date Extended…" 180K
- A recurring monthly series, posted on the 1st. Only rules confirmed by official notifications.

**15. "Sangam Market, Sagarpur: दुकानदारों के 3 सवाल"**
- Local proof, street-interview style (with consent): 3 real shopkeeper questions answered on the spot.
- The format no big creator can copy, and it feeds the Market Cluster loop.
