## Design notes
- **Hierarchy:** headline → 3 checks/steps → QR. Someone should get it in 3 seconds from 1 metre away.
- **Text size:** Devanagari headline ≥ 28 pt; body ≥ 10 pt; footer ≥ 7 pt. Use a Devanagari font with clear matras (e.g. Noto Sans Devanagari / Mukta). Font is TODO in brand.md.
- **Colours:** navy #0A1E3F base, teal #22D3A6 for the price band, cyan #00B5E2 accent on the back, mint #A8FAC9 for the health-card dots background. Keep one colour per idea.
- **White space:** if it feels crowded, cut the document checklist from the back first (it can go into the WhatsApp quick reply).
- **Logo** + "Your Path to Tax Clarity" small at the top or footer, not both.

## Before print (checklist)
1. ☐ **CA Prateek Arora** confirms the 31 Dec 2026 belated ITR date for AY 2026-27 and the line "31 Dec ke baad belated ITR ka option nahi rehta" (updated return / ITR-U is a separate route; the wording must not mislead) [verify].
2. ☐ Owner sets the **ITR price** (offers file proposes ₹999 ITR-1/2 and ₹1,999 ITR-3/4 [Estimate, not approved]). If there are 2 prices, print "from ₹___".
3. ☐ Owner confirms the team can deliver the health check in ~15 min and set the **weekly slot cap** (offers file suggests 15/week). Remove "Limited slots per week" if there's no cap.
4. ☐ Fill in **office hours**.
5. ☐ A Hindi speaker proofreads the Devanagari (matras, spelling of "प्रतीक अरोड़ा").
6. ☐ Both QRs test-scanned; the WhatsApp Business quick replies for "Health Check" and "ITR" are ready before the first flyer goes out.

**Deliberately left out:** the service guarantee and the referral offer (not yet cleared by CA Prateek under ICAI norms), any late-fee amounts (law-specific, needs a CA check), and any client count or review claims (none exist yet).
