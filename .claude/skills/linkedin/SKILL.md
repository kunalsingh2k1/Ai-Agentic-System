## Process
1. **Profile/page audit**: headline (who you help + outcome), banner, About (story → proof → CTA), featured section, company page tagline and CTA button.
2. **Content system**: 3 pillars (expertise, story, proof), formats (text posts, document/carousel PDFs, short video, polls, newsletters), cadence 3–5×/week.
3. **Post craft**: hook in the first 2 lines (before "…see more"), short lines, one idea, concrete story/data, soft CTA or question. External links in the first comment.
4. **Engagement routine**: comment meaningfully on 10 ICP/creator posts daily; reply to all comments in the first hour.

## Output
Rewritten headline/About options, pillar plan, 10 ready posts (with hook variants), 1 carousel outline, weekly routine checklist.
