## Process
1. Goal (awareness, content for ads, sales) → creator tier (nano 1–10k, micro 10–100k, mid 100–500k, macro) and platform.
2. Find creators: hashtags, competitor tags, "who the audience follows", creator marketplaces. Vet: real engagement rate, audience geography/age fit, brand safety, past sponsorship performance.
3. Deal structure: gifting, flat fee, affiliate %, hybrid; usage rights for ads (whitelisting/Spark Ads); disclosure (#ad / paid partnership label).
4. Brief: key message, must-say/must-not-say, creative freedom, deliverables, timeline, tracking (code/UTM).

## Output
Program plan, creator scoring rubric, shortlist template, outreach DM/email drafts (draft only), creator brief, tracking sheet columns, budget and expected-return model.
